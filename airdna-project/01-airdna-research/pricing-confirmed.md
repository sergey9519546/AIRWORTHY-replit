# AirDNA Pricing — Verified 2026

> Sources: Awning review (Apr 2026), AirDNA website, HBS case study (Nov 2022)

---

## Current Pricing (2026)

| Plan | Price | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0/mo | Basic market overview, limited data |
| **Starter** | **~$15/mo** | Individual property lookup, revenue estimates |
| **Professional** | **~$25-40/mo** | Full market analytics, historical data, exports |
| **Rentalizer** | **~$19.95/report** | Per-property revenue estimate (one-time) |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | API access, bulk data, team accounts, custom reporting |

> Source: Awning review, April 2026: "The Starter plan at roughly $15 per month... The Professional plan at $25 to $40 per month"
