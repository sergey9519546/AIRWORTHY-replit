## Founding Story
- **Founded:** 2014 by Scott Shatford (CEO) and Will Shatford
- **HQ:** 1321 15th St. Denver, CO 80202
- **Europe office:** Carrer de Muntaner 292 6.2, 08021 Barcelona, Spain
- **NYC office:** 33 East 33rd Street, Suite 900, New York, NY 10016
- **Origin:** Scott rented his Santa Monica place on Airbnb while traveling in SE Asia, earned more than expected, saw data gap
- **Background:** Scott spent 9 years managing a BI team at Korn/Ferry International
- **First product:** E-book "The Airbnb Expert's Playbook" (500K+ downloads)
- **Second product:** MarketMinder analytics dashboard
- **Never raised VC** — bootstrapped until acquisition (confirmed via PR Newswire)

## Acquisition
- **Acquirer:** Alpine Investors (private equity firm)
- **Date:** March 14, 2022
- **Terms:** Not disclosed
- **Context:** Second STR data acquisition in two weeks — OTA Insight had just purchased Transparent Intelligence
- **Post-acquisition:** Continues operating independently
